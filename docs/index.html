中小学知识图谱隐私政策

更新日期：2026-10-27 | 生效日期：2026-10-27

欢迎使用中小学知识图谱（CnMathTaxonomy）。本政策适用于中小学知识图谱 HarmonyOS 版本在安装、使用等过程中对信息的处理方式。开发者/运营者为本应用的发布主体，具体以华为应用市场页面公示的实名认证主体为准；联系邮箱为 tomkuku588@gmail.com。本应用是一款完全离线运行的学科知识自查工具，免费、无广告、无需注册。

1. 我们处理的信息范围及用途

我们处理的信息仅为你在使用过程中在你设备本地主动创建或自动生成的学习数据，包括：

  • 诊断会话：你选定的知识点范围、学段/年级与题量；
  • 逐题作答记录与判分结果；
  • 错题记录与知识点收藏；
  • 偏好设置：当前学科、深浅色模式、首次启动声明确认状态；
  • 你主动导出的 JSON 备份文件，以及为展示报告而生成的本地统计与临时图片缓存。

处理这些信息的目的在于实现知识图谱浏览、学情诊断、诊断报告展示、错题重练、收藏管理与学习进度呈现，以及必要的故障排查。当前版本不提供账号注册或登录，不收集姓名、手机号、电子邮箱、精准位置信息、通讯录、短信、通话记录、相册内容、人脸、指纹、设备标识符等个人信息，也不具备采集上述信息的技术条件。

2. 信息存储地点、期限与删除

上述信息默认存储在你的设备本地应用沙箱内的数据库与偏好设置文件中，不会上传至开发者服务器或任何第三方服务，当前版本也不提供云同步。信息保存期限取决于你的使用行为。当你删除单条诊断历史、清空错题本、移除收藏、清空应用数据、卸载应用或重置设备后，相应数据将在可控范围内被删除。若你将应用导出的备份文件另行复制到其他设备、云盘或第三方服务，相关存储行为由你自行决定并负责。

3. 权限与系统能力说明

当前版本的核心功能在完全离线的状态下即可运行。应用未声明、未申请任何系统权限，其中包括网络访问权限；因此应用无法主动连接互联网，也不会以通知、定位、相机、麦克风、通讯录、短信等方式获取信息。

为实现数据备份与恢复，应用调用 HarmonyOS 提供的系统文件选择与文件保存能力（DocumentViewPicker），仅用于完成你明确发起的导出或导入操作，可读写的具体文件由你在系统选择器中选定，应用不自行扫描或遍历设备文件。为实现诊断报告分享图保存，仅在你点击「保存分享图」时，通过 HarmonyOS 安全控件（SaveButton）触发一次性的相册写入临时授权；你拒绝该授权不影响任何其他功能。除实现前述功能所必需的系统能力外，当前版本不以后台方式读取无关文件，也不采集与学习自查无关的个人信息。若后续版本新增权限或系统能力需求，我们会在相应功能触发前另行说明，并依法取得相应授权。

4. 信息共享、转让与公开披露

我们不会将你的个人信息出售、出租、交换或无关共享给第三方，也不会擅自公开披露。仅在以下情形下可能发生信息对外流转：你主动使用导出备份、导入备份、保存分享图等能力；法律法规、监管机关或司法机关要求；或者为保护你、其他用户或公众的人身财产安全所必需。在上述情形下，我们会在法律允许的范围内采取合理保护措施。

5. 第三方 SDK、开源数据与对外服务

除 HarmonyOS 系统基础能力外，当前版本不接入广告、商业化统计分析、第三方登录、地图、支付、推送等第三方 SDK，也不向开发者自建服务器或任何外部服务上传你的作答记录、错题、收藏或设置数据。应用内学情诊断的判分、掌握度评估与学习路径建议，均由设备本地的规则与算法引擎计算得出，不调用任何云端 AI 服务，也不上传题目或作答内容。

应用内置的知识点、先修关系与题目数据来自开源项目 cn-math-taxonomy，采用 CC BY-NC 4.0（署名-非商业性使用 4.0 国际）许可，已在应用内按许可要求署名。该数据为 AI 生成初稿并经人工整理，未经教师全面审核，可能存在错误、遗漏或过时表述，仅供学习自查参考。若后续版本引入联网能力或其他第三方服务，我们会同步更新本政策，并在必要时通过弹窗、页面提示或系统授权流程取得你的明示同意。

6. 信息安全

我们会采取与当前产品形态相适应的合理安全措施，包括完全本地化处理、最小必要原则、仅在你发起操作时访问文件等，以降低信息被未经授权访问、披露、篡改或丢失的风险。请你理解，任何技术措施都无法保证绝对安全。

本地学习数据未做加密存储：若你的设备交由他人使用或丢失，对方可能直接看到应用内的作答记录与诊断报告；本应用不提供设备级锁或独立密码。你仍需妥善保管自己的设备、系统账号以及你自行导出的备份文件。

7. 你的权利

你可以在应用内查看、修改、删除诊断历史、错题与收藏，可以在设置中导出全部本地数据为 JSON 备份文件用于自查与迁移，也可以清空应用数据或卸载应用以删除全部信息。由于当前版本不提供在线账号系统，访问、更正、删除、撤回同意等操作可以直接在设备本地完成，无需向我们提出请求；如果你对本政策、数据处理或权限使用有疑问，可以通过本政策载明的联系邮箱与我们联系。

8. 未成年人保护

本应用面向中小学生设计，未成年人是主要使用群体。若你是未满 14 周岁的未成年人，应在监护人阅读并同意本政策后使用本应用；若你是未满 8 周岁的儿童，应在监护人陪同与指导下使用。由于应用不联网、不收集、不上传任何个人信息，未成年人使用本应用不会产生个人信息对外流转的风险。我们不会面向未成年人提供定向营销、内购、社交或内容推荐服务，也建议监护人关注孩子的设备使用时长与用眼健康。

CnMathTaxonomy Privacy Policy

Welcome to CnMathTaxonomy (K-12 Knowledge Graph). This policy applies to the HarmonyOS version of the app and explains how information is handled when you install and use it. The developer/operator is the entity publishing the app, as displayed on the AppGallery listing under the verified developer identity. The contact email is tomkuku588@gmail.com. The app is a fully offline study self-check tool that is free, ad-free, and requires no registration.

1. What Information We Process and Why

We process only the study data that you actively create or that is generated locally on your device while you use the app:

  • diagnosis sessions: the knowledge points, school stage/grade, and question count you select;
  • per-question answer records and scoring results;
  • mistake records and knowledge-point favorites;
  • preference settings: current subject, light/dark appearance, and the first-launch disclaimer acknowledgement;
  • JSON backup files that you export yourself, plus temporary local statistics and image caches generated to display a report.

These are processed to provide knowledge-graph browsing, learning diagnosis, report display, mistake re-practice, favorites management, progress display, and necessary troubleshooting. The current version does not provide account registration or login. It does not collect your name, phone number, email address, precise location, contacts, SMS, call logs, photo library contents, facial data, fingerprints, device identifiers, or similar personal information, and it has no technical means to do so.

2. Storage Location, Retention, and Deletion

The above information is stored by default in a database and preference files inside the app sandbox on your device. It is not uploaded to any developer-operated server or any third-party service, and the current version does not provide cloud sync. Retention depends on your own actions. The relevant data can be removed when you delete a diagnosis history entry, clear the mistake book, remove favorites, clear app data, uninstall the app, or reset the device. If you later copy exported backup files generated by the app to another device, cloud drive, or third-party service, that storage is controlled by you.

3. Permissions and System Capabilities

The core features of the current version run fully offline. The app declares and requests no system permissions at all, including network access; it therefore cannot connect to the internet and does not obtain information through notifications, location, camera, microphone, contacts, or SMS.

For backup and restore, the app invokes HarmonyOS system file picking and file saving capabilities (DocumentViewPicker) solely to complete the export or import action you initiated; the specific file that is read or written is the one you choose in the system picker, and the app does not scan or traverse your device files on its own. For saving a report share image, a one-time temporary album write authorization is triggered through the HarmonyOS security control (SaveButton) only when you tap "Save share image"; declining it does not affect any other feature. Other than what is necessary for those functions, the current version does not read unrelated files in the background and does not collect personal information unrelated to study self-check. If a future version adds new permissions or system capabilities, we will explain them before the relevant feature is triggered and obtain authorization where required by law.

4. Sharing, Transfer, and Public Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your personal information with unrelated third parties, and we do not publicly disclose it without authorization. External transfer may occur only when you actively use export, import, or save-share-image features; when required by laws, regulators, or courts; or when necessary to protect the personal or property safety of you, other users, or the public. In such cases, we will take reasonable protective measures within the scope permitted by law.

5. Third-Party SDKs, Open Source Data, and External Services

Except for HarmonyOS base system capabilities, the current version does not integrate third-party SDKs for advertising, commercial analytics, social login, maps, payment, or push notifications, and it does not upload your answer records, mistakes, favorites, or settings to any developer-operated server or external service. In-app scoring, mastery estimation, and learning-path suggestions are computed by a rule and algorithm engine on your device. The app does not call any cloud AI service and does not upload questions or answers.

The built-in knowledge points, prerequisite relations, and questions come from the open-source project cn-math-taxonomy, licensed under CC BY-NC 4.0 (Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International), and are attributed in the app as the license requires. That data is an AI-generated draft that has been manually organized but has not been fully reviewed by teachers, and may contain errors, omissions, or outdated wording; it is provided for study self-check reference only. If a future version introduces network capabilities or other third-party services, we will update this policy and, where necessary, obtain your explicit consent through notices, dialogs, or system authorization flows.

6. Data Security

We adopt reasonable safeguards appropriate to the current product form, including fully local processing, the minimum-necessary principle, and accessing files only when you initiate an action, in order to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss. Please understand that no technical measure can guarantee absolute security.

Local study data is not stored encrypted: if your device is used by or lost to others, they may directly view your answer records and diagnosis reports in the app. The app does not provide a device lock or a separate passcode. You remain responsible for the security of your device, system accounts, and any backup files you export.

7. Your Rights

You can view, edit, or delete diagnosis history, mistakes, and favorites in the app; export all local data as a JSON backup file from the settings page for your own review and migration; or clear app data and uninstall the app to remove all information. Because the current version does not provide an online account system, rights such as access, correction, deletion, and withdrawal of consent can be exercised locally on the device without a request to us. If you have questions about this policy, data handling, or permission use, you may contact us through the email provided above.

If you are under the age of 14, you should use the app only after your parent or legal guardian has read and agreed to this policy. If you are under the age of 8, you should use the app accompanied and guided by a guardian. Because the app does not connect to the network and does not collect or upload any personal information, use by minors creates no risk of personal information leaving the device. We do not provide targeted marketing, in-app purchases, social features, or content recommendation toward minors. We encourage guardians to pay attention to children's screen time and eye health.

9. Updates and Contact

If there is a material change to product features, processing purposes, data types, permission use, or applicable rules (for example, introducing network capability), we will update this policy and provide notice in the app and on a prominent position of this page. The latest version of this policy is always published on this page. You may contact us at tomkuku588@gmail.com, or visit the GitHub repository at https://github.com/tomkuku588-bot/mathtaxonomy.
